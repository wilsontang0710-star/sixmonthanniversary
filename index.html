OUR STORY

Six little chapters.

Six months of becoming us.

26.03.2026
CHAPTER I

The beginning.

It started with something so small — I added you, and you were the one who asked me, “Do we know each other?”

I don't think either of us knew where that little conversation would lead us.

One message became another. Then another.

And somewhere between those conversations, you slowly stopped being just someone I had met online.

You became someone I wanted to know. Someone I wanted to hear from. Someone I started looking forward to every day.

And somehow, that simple beginning became the first page of our story.

Six months later, I'm still here, writing about the girl who once asked me if we knew each other.

APRIL
CHAPTER II

Getting closer.

We started trying to understand the person behind the screen.

We talked about things that went deeper than simple conversations. We asked questions, shared little pieces of ourselves, and slowly learned how the other person thinks, feels, and sees the world.

Then came our first call.

I still remember how shy and awkward it felt. Different languages, different places, and two people who were still trying to figure each other out.

But then I heard your voice.

And somehow, all those differences felt a little smaller.

I didn't need to understand every word perfectly. Just hearing you there made me happy.

That was when you started becoming more than just words on my screen.

MAY
CHAPTER III

Somewhere between.

Somewhere along the way, we started becoming part of each other's everyday lives.

We began staying on calls, sometimes talking until we were tired, sometimes simply being there without needing to say much.

I loved hearing the little things about your day. The random things you told me, the cute things you said, the small details that probably meant nothing to anyone else but somehow became important to me.

Even with the language between us, I never got tired of hearing your voice.

May also brought our first little arguments.

We didn't always understand each other. Sometimes our words came out wrong, sometimes our feelings became bigger than what we were trying to say.

But every time, we found our way back to each other.

And every time we did, I learned something new about you.

Maybe that's what getting closer really meant — not never hurting each other, but slowly learning how to understand each other's hearts.

And with every conversation, every argument, every late night, you became a little more familiar to me.

A little more mine.

JUNE
CHAPTER IV

Closer than distance.

By June, calling you had quietly become part of my everyday life.

We started falling asleep on calls together, waking up knowing the other person was still there.

Sometimes we talked. Sometimes we were both doing our own things. Sometimes we just stayed connected in silence.

And every once in a while, I got to see your face through a video call instead of only imagining you from the other side of the screen.

We also became physically and sexually intimate with each other for the first time.

It was something new for both of us — something private, vulnerable, and deeply personal.

But what made those moments special to me wasn't simply the intimacy itself.

It was the trust behind it.

The feeling that, despite all the distance between Malaysia and Kazakhstan, there was still a part of our relationship that belonged only to us.

We could be open with each other, show each other sides of ourselves that we didn't show everyone else, and trust each other with something so personal.

For the first time, the distance didn't feel quite so impossible.

You were still far away. But somehow, you felt closer than ever.

JULY — AUGUST
CHAPTER V

Through everything.

By then, we had already been through so much together.

There were beautiful days. There were difficult days. There were arguments, misunderstandings, jealousy, laughter, and moments when loving each other wasn't always easy.

But somehow, every part of it became part of our story.

The good moments gave me happiness. The difficult ones taught me more about you, and more about myself.

And then you were there for my birthday.

It might have been just another birthday to the rest of the world, but having the person I love there with me made it feel different.

Even though you weren't physically beside me, I still felt like I was celebrating it with you.

That moment meant more to me than I probably knew how to say at the time.

Because sometimes happiness isn't about having everything you want.

Sometimes it's simply about having the person you want there with you.

And after everything we've been through, I still found myself choosing to stay, to understand, to love you a little more.

Even when things weren't always easy,
we were still here — still talking,
still trying, still finding our way back to each other.

Maybe that's what makes these months ours.
26.09.2026
CHAPTER VI

Six months.

Six months.

When I write it down, it doesn't sound like such a long time.

But when I think about everything inside those six months, it feels like we've lived through so many little worlds together.

From one simple “Do we know each other?” to late-night calls, sleepy voices, stupid arguments, little jokes, quiet moments, birthdays, and the intimate moments we shared in private — all the things that slowly became ours.

Different countries. Different time zones. Different lives.

And somehow, you became part of mine.

I don't know exactly what the next chapter will look like.

I don't know how many more difficult days we'll have, how many more things we'll learn about each other, or where life will eventually take us.

But I know that I don't want these six months to be just a memory.

I want to keep turning the pages.

Because six months ago, you were just someone who asked me if we knew each other.

Now, you're someone whose voice I look for, whose presence became part of my everyday life, and someone I can't simply describe as someone I met.

You're part of my story now.

And maybe that's the most beautiful thing about all of this —

our story is only six chapters in,
and there are still so many pages left to write.

Happy six months, Ulya.

This isn't the end of our story.
It's just another page.
